# Bible Reader v0.1.4 - Native offline voice reader and karaoke
- Press X on any verse for TTS, then read continuously across verses, chapters and books until O Stop.
- Highlight the actually synthesized spoken word in karaoke yellow in sync with the SDL audio queue and eSpeak NG word events.
- English, German, French and Spanish offline voice dictionaries bundled from GPL-3.0 eSpeak NG 1.52.0.
- Audio plays through the proven PS5 SDL2 backend used by Audiobook Reader.
- Existing multilingual offline Bibles, full-width reading, app icon, bookmarks, language preferences and Title ID remain intact.
- Hardware testing required before calling speech or sync verified on PS5.
